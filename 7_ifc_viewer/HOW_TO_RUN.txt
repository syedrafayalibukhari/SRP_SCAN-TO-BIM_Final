STEP 7 - IFC VIEWER
===================

What it does
  ifc_viewer.html shows and edits the IFC model in the browser. It needs no installation. Open it
  in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge with an internet connection, which it uses to load its 3D and
  IFC libraries.

1. Double-click ifc_viewer.html.
2. Click "Choose IFC file" and open ../5_modeling/output/model.ifc.

The model also opens in any other IFC viewer, for example BIMvision, Solibri Anywhere, Bonsai
(BlenderBIM) or Autodesk Revit.
